  1. 1 · StandbyCylinders hold liquid FM200, pushed by nitrogen at 25 bar (360 psi). Panel normal.
  2. 2 · First alarmOne smoke detector (Zone 1) sees smoke. Alarm bell rings. Nothing is released yet.
  3. 3 · Second alarmZone 2 confirms (cross-zone). Sounder & strobe, HVAC off, countdown starts. Leave the room.
  4. 4 · ReleasePanel fires the solenoid on the master cylinder. Pilot pressure opens the slave valve.
  5. 5 · Through the pipesAgent flows down the hoses, through check valves into the manifold and along the pipe network.
  6. 6 · Nozzle dischargeLiquid flashes to gas at the 360° nozzles and fills the room within 10 seconds.
  7. 7 · Fire outAbout 7% concentration puts the fire out. Keep the room closed for the hold time, then ventilate.

Training model, not a design tool. Real FM200 is colourless; it is shown as white mist here. The flow is slowed down so you can follow it: in a real system the agent reaches the nozzles in about a second and the whole discharge is over in 10 seconds or less (NFPA 2001).
